# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a safe, respectful, inclusive, collaborative, and productive space where people of all backgrounds can contribute and learn. We welcome people regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Everyone participating in project discussions, reviews, activities, or affiliated community spaces is expected to help maintain that environment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and focused on the work.
- Be open to different backgrounds, experiences, identities, skills, and approaches.
- Respect others' time, privacy, and boundaries.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language and avoid assumptions about other people's identity or experience.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, learn from feedback, and help repair harm.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, listen to feedback, and correct harm where possible.
- Protect confidential information and do not share private contact details or sensitive project discussions without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment or discrimination based on any protected or personal characteristic, sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention, intimidation, stalking, threats or violent language, deliberate humiliation or personal attacks, trolling, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Disagreement about project decisions is welcome when expressed respectfully. Criticism should address ideas, evidence, or work, not a person's identity or worth.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, documentation, presentations, and project-related events or communications. It also applies when someone is representing the project or when conduct materially affects the safety or inclusion of project participants.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact one or more project maintainers privately through the team's established course communication channel or GitHub direct messaging. If the concern involves a project maintainer, contact another maintainer or the course teaching team so the report can be reviewed without a conflict of interest.

Include relevant context, links, dates, and names or usernames when possible. Do not post private or sensitive details in a public issue. If immediate safety or course conduct procedures are involved, also contact the appropriate course or university authority.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. Information may be shared with people who need it to review the concern or take action. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## How concerns are handled

Project maintainers will listen to the reporter, review the available context, and consult other maintainers or course staff when needed. They will act promptly and proportionately, taking the report seriously even when they do not observe the conduct themselves.

Consequences will depend on the severity and repetition of the behavior. They may include a private correction or reminder, a formal warning, a requirement to repair harm, removal of harmful content, restricted participation in project spaces, removal from the team or repository, or referral to course or university conduct processes. Serious or repeated behavior may result in immediate removal from project spaces.

All maintainers are expected to follow this policy. If you disagree with how a report was handled, you may raise the concern privately with another maintainer or the course teaching team.
